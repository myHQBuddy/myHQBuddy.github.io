5.2 · Urgency vs pressure

Helping them decide, not forcing them.

Urgency helps the customer decide. Pressure makes the customer feel forced. The line between them is whether the information you gave was true.

Healthy urgency
  • Explain genuine deadlines or verified limited availability
  • Highlight the benefit of timely, well-informed action
  • Give full clarity before asking for a decision
  • Allow an informed, autonomous choice
Pressure selling
  • Creating false or misleading urgency
  • Repeatedly pushing for payment without resolving doubts
  • Using fear or misleading statements to force a closure
  • Pushing a decision without sufficient information
Consequence. Formal warning plus performance review on repeat occurrence.

Sales conduct and standards

Six non-negotiable standards

Every customer interaction builds or breaks trust. Customers rely on us for correct information, and one wrong commitment affects trust, refunds and compliance at once. Responsible selling protects the customer, the brand and every representative. The six standards below are minimum requirements, not good-to-have qualities.

1. Professional communication. A calm, respectful tone even with difficult customers. No sarcasm, dismissiveness, impatience or casual shortcuts. Every interaction reflects myHQ.

2. Active listening. Do not interrupt; let the customer complete their statement. Confirm understanding by paraphrasing the need before responding — for example, “Just to confirm, you are looking for a virtual office address mainly for GST registration in Delhi NCR, correct?” Active listening directly reduces wrong pitching and refund risk.

3. Recommend based on need, not pressure. Base recommendations on the customer’s requirement and use case, their location preference and compliance need, and the correct plan fit. Never on commission or quota pressure, personal preference, or a fast-closure mindset. Selling the wrong plan is not sales excellence — it is a compliance risk.

4. Transparency and full disclosure before payment. Pricing and plan limits, applicable policies, location restrictions and documentation requirements all get stated before the customer pays.

5. Accuracy is mandatory. Representatives must know the Virtual Office plans and space types, documentation requirements, process timelines and eligibility, and applicable policies and restrictions. If unsure — pause, verify, respond accurately. Never guess.

6. Help the customer decide confidently. Anticipate questions and address concerns before they arise, explain clearly what happens after payment, and build confidence. A well-informed customer converts, stays and refers.

Omission counts as misinformation. Omitting important information is treated as seriously as giving false information.

Zero-tolerance violations

Most conduct issues are coached first. Three are not.

Mis-selling includes incorrect or exaggerated information, hiding limitations, making unverified promises, and selling a plan that does not fit the customer’s need. The impact is refund disputes, compliance risk, brand damage and lost trust. The consequence is immediate escalation and disciplinary action, up to termination.

Rudeness and disrespect covers a dismissive, aggressive, impatient or disrespectful tone in any interaction, interrupting the customer or making them feel unheard, and responding defensively instead of seeking to understand. The consequence is a formal warning from the first occurrence, depending on severity.

Data breach means sharing customer or company data without authorisation, unauthorised access, loss or exposure of sensitive information, or failing to uphold confidentiality protocols. The response is immediate suspension pending investigation, a complete audit of data access, possible legal action, and termination as a potential outcome.

Separately, unprofessional communication — poor grammar, unclear phrasing, emojis or informal language in formal customer channels, and unverifiable claims — is coached first, with a formal warning on repeat occurrence.

Urgency is not pressure selling

Healthy urgency means explaining genuine deadlines or verified limited availability, highlighting the benefit of timely action, giving full clarity before asking for a decision, and allowing an informed, autonomous choice. Pressure selling means creating false or misleading urgency, repeatedly pushing for payment without resolving doubts, using fear or misleading statements to force a closure, and pushing a decision without sufficient information.

Urgency helps the customer decide. Pressure makes the customer feel forced. The consequence for pressure selling is a formal warning plus performance review on repeat occurrence.
