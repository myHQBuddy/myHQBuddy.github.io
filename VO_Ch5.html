07

Sales, payment and refunds

The operational flow

Every Virtual Office sale follows a structured three-step process, and consistency and speed at each stage directly affect conversion.

Step 1 — the lead arrives on Zoho CRM. Leads come from paid ads, social media, SEO or the website, and are auto-assigned to a sales agent with name, email, mobile, city and space preference.

Step 2 — contact within 15 minutes. The sooner you call, the higher the conversion rate. Understand the requirement, pitch the right plan, and listen more than you speak.

Step 3 — send the proposal and follow up. Send the proposal emailer, update the CRM, set a follow-up date, and keep following up proactively via calls and emails until closure. The proposal itself gives the customer the selected plan, pricing breakup, applicable charges, documentation details, location information, next steps, inclusions, terms and conditions, refund policy and support information — a transparent decision-making document before payment.

How pricing is built

Every plan is priced as base price + payment gateway charges + documentation charges + 18% GST on the total. Discounts apply only on the base price — always clarify this upfront, because a client who assumes the discount applies to the full amount will dispute the final invoice.

Payment routes

Online payment is preferred. It runs through the myHQ website using a payment link you create, coupon codes map instantly, and payment is confirmed immediately with no manual follow-up. Offline or manual invoicing is reserved for large companies that insist on it; payment arrives via NEFT, RTGS or IMPS, and there is typically a 24–48 hour delay before the subscription activates. After payment, fill the VO Transaction Form immediately to ensure accurate billing, onboarding and fulfilment.

Refund policy

A refund is possible before the draft agreement has been signed by the client and approved by the space, or after two government rejections provided the rejection letter is submitted within 60 days of payment. Where eligible, the refund is processed minus a ₹2,000 operational charge.

A refund is not given after the agreement is signed by both parties, if the client does not complete KYC within 30 days and fails to inform us, or if errors in the application were made by the client. Payment gateway charges and documentation charges are never refunded, regardless of the reason.

The process: the client raises a ticket at supportvo@myhq.in with the reason and a cancelled cheque for bank verification; the operations team reviews eligibility within 48–72 hours; approved refunds are credited within 21 working days to the client’s bank account or original payment source.

08

Fulfilment and KYC

From payment to delivered documents

Fulfilment begins the moment payment is received. The client receives an automatic email with login instructions, logs in at myHQ.in/virtual-office using their payment email ID, and finds their subscription under My Account → Virtual Office → Subscriptions.

From there, one of two paths applies depending on the space — and this expectation needs setting during the sale, not after it.

The ~15 minute path

Upload KYC with business documents and details, review the auto-generated agreement, sign digitally, and receive notarised e-stamp documents. Digital, automated and effectively instant.

The 4–5 working day path

Upload KYC with business documents and details, complete KYC with director and signatory details, the space reviews and approves the KYC, the agreement is generated, signed and uploaded, and the space stamps, notarises and uploads the final documents. This applies to non-WeWork spaces, and the timeline runs from KYC completion rather than from payment.

The 30-day rule. KYC must be completed within 30 days of payment. After this window the client is no longer eligible for a refund under any circumstances.

The specific documents required from the client vary by entity type — Private Limited, LLP, OPC, proprietor or partnership firm, and whether the company is new or already registered. That checklist is covered in detail in the Sales Pitch chapter.

09

Government verification

What happens after documents are shared

Once clients receive their Virtual Office documents, they apply for company registration via mca.gov.in or GST registration via gst.gov.in. Several things may follow.

A government clarification notice may ask for more documents, which the client uploads via the dashboard or raises as a support ticket. An on-ground verification may see officials visit the space to verify documents and photograph signage — the space handles this, and in most cases no action is needed from the client. Physical verification requires a company representative to be present; neither myHQ nor the space can impersonate the client, and this must be communicated clearly during the sales process rather than discovered on the day. Throughout the year, mail and packages arrive at the space for collection in person or courier delivery.

What changed in GST 2.0

Registrations are no longer approved on basic document submission alone. Verification is now risk-based and validation-driven, taking into account the nature of the business, address authenticity, the applicant’s profile and filing behaviour, and document consistency and digital validation. There is no fixed scrutiny checklist — verification depends on the authority’s risk assessment.

The three verification types

TypeHow it is doneWhat authorities checkRisk triggers
DocumentReview of submitted registration documents and address proofsNOC, agreement, utility bill, name and address consistencyMismatch in company name, incomplete documents, address inconsistencies
DigitalOnline and backend validation of business presence and traceabilityGoogle Maps location, website and social media, contact responsiveness, linked filingsWeak digital footprint, inactive contact details, suspicious registration patterns
PhysicalA GST officer physically visits the registered addressSignage, occupancy, business activity proof, authorisation to use the addressShared addresses, inaccessible premises, non-availability during inspection

What authorities check, at each level

Customer level: company registration details are verified with MCA, business activity and projected turnover are reviewed for GST eligibility, the authorised signatory’s identity and authority are confirmed, and understanding of GST obligations — filing returns, paying taxes, maintaining records — is assessed.

Space level: the legitimacy of the coworking space is verified via utility bills and property agreements, subleasing authorisation is confirmed as properly documented, the NOC from the property owner must be in place, and for leased spaces the Master Agreement between space and building owner is validated.

How different spaces handle verification

WeWork spaces use a fully system-based approach. No physical signage is displayed; WeWork uses open areas, phone booths and meeting rooms to represent Virtual Office companies during government verification. Auto-approval gives a faster turnaround — a minimum of 15 minutes post-KYC in most cases.

Non-WeWork spaces display signage at reception showing company names and GST numbers, reserve designated areas or floors for Virtual Office companies, and during GST verification drives authorities verify active legal agreements rather than requiring physical occupancy. The timeline is 4–5 working days post-KYC completion.

Both approaches are government-accepted. What matters is that documents are complete, the address is active, and signage — where required — is properly displayed before any verification visit.

Geo-tagged images and bank account verification

Clients can request geo-tagged images of the location, signage or verification setup when required for GST or banking compliance. Raise a ticket at supportvo@myhq.in with the client name, subscription ID and the specific image requirement; the ops team coordinates with the space, subject to partner availability.

For bank account opening, banks commonly require the Certificate of Incorporation, company PAN, GST certificate where available, MOA and AOA, a board resolution for account opening, director PAN and Aadhaar, and office address proof — which for a Virtual Office means the agreement, NOC and utility bill. Verification may be document-based, digital (MCA records, GST check, website presence, email and phone validation), or physical and video-based (video KYC, geo-tagged office images, signage verification, and in some cases a physical visit).

10

Support and escalation

Who to contact, and when

Support runs through two channels: supportvo@myhq.in for non-WeWork queries and contactwwvo@myhq.in for WeWork queries. Tickets are handled in Zoho Desk and auto-assigned round-robin to an operations POC.

First response is due within 1 hour. If it is delayed, Level 1 escalation goes to Ankit for WeWork or Ayush for non-WeWork, and then to Shashank.

Resolution is due within 2 days. If the ticket remains unresolved, it escalates to Prakher.

No ticket is closed without proper resolution, and any negative feedback is escalated to management — Nitin and Prakher.

Know this ladder before you need it. The most common cause of a support complaint becoming an escalation is a representative who did not know where to send it and let it sit.
