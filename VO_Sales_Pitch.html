Overview

Why this framework exists

This chapter is the official Sales Pitch Framework for myHQ’s Virtual Office team. It standardises how every client interaction runs, from the opening call through to post-payment handoff.

Two principles sit underneath all of it. Personalisation means tailoring the conversation to the individual client’s context and requirements rather than reciting a script at them. Consultative selling means understanding the business need before recommending a solution — which in practice means the discovery stages matter more than the pitch.

If you only remember one rule: never pitch a plan before you have confirmed the use case. Every avoidable lost deal in VO traces back to that.

The seven plan paths

Follow only the path that matches

Based on the general RCA, route the client to the correct plan-specific discovery path. Each path has its own follow-up questions and exactly one recommended plan. Do not blend paths.

Path 1 — Company Registration

Trigger. Client is setting up a new company and needs a registered address for MCA.

Have you completed MCA name approval (the RUN process)?
What type of company are you registering?
Are all directors Indian residents, or are there foreign nationals or NRIs?
Will you also need GST registration at this address?
Do you have a CA or CS who will file the incorporation?

Recommend: Company Registration Plan

Path 2 — GST Registration

Trigger. Existing registered company needs GST in a new city or state.

What is your company’s state of incorporation?
In which states do you need GST registered?
Do you supply goods or services to customers in this state?
Have you obtained GST in this state before?
Does your CA have experience working with virtual office addresses?

Recommend: GST Registration Plan

Path 3 — Business Address Only

Trigger. Needs a professional address for website, letterheads, cards or Google My Business.

Is this address purely for correspondence, or also for government filing?
Will you list this address on your website, visiting cards or Google My Business?
Are you a freelancer, sole proprietor, or an established company expanding presence?
Do you need one city or multiple cities?

Recommend: Business Address Plan

Path 4 — Registered Address Change

Trigger. Wants to change an existing company’s registered address on MCA and bank accounts.

Where is your company currently registered?
Why do you want to change the registered address on MCA or other licences?
Will you also need to update GST at the new address?
Is your CA handling the change on the MCA portal?

Recommend: Company Registration Plan

Path 5 — GST Address Change

Trigger. Changing the address on an existing GST registration, same or new state.

Are you changing the principal place of business, or adding an additional place?
Is this a state change, or an address change within the same state?

Recommend: GST Registration Plan

Path 6 — VPOB & APOB

Trigger. Sells on Amazon, Flipkart or Meesho and needs a Virtual Place of Business.

Which marketplace are you selling on?
Is the marketplace requiring GST in a specific state for their fulfilment centres?
Has the marketplace shared a specific list of documents they require?

Recommend: GST Plan — verify with compliance

Path 7 — Miscellaneous

Trigger. FSSAI, RERA, Trade Licence, Trust, MSME/Udyam or IEC registrations.

FSSAI licence — verify space compatibility before selling
RERA licence — requires specific documentation
Trade Licence / S&E Certificate — verify by city
Trust registration — confirm space support
MSME / Udyam — generally supported, low risk
IEC (Import Export Code) — address must match the VO address

Recommend: Confirm with your TL first

Path 1. Do not recommend the GST Plan or Business Address Plan for a new company incorporation. The documentation will not satisfy MCA and the application will be rejected.
Path 6, VPOB/APOB. Requirements vary by marketplace and change often. Always verify with the compliance team before confirming anything to the client.
Path 4. Address-change clients often underestimate that they need the top-tier plan. Educate rather than discount.
Path 7. Tell the client: “We support this in many locations, but I want to verify with our team before I confirm.”
05

Customer types

One strategy, different pitch

The sales stream never changes: understand the context, identify the need, position the solution, build the value. What changes is the reason you give this particular client to buy.

Time-based — “I need this done fast”

Lead with turnaround. Fast documentation, quick KYC and onboarding, verified locations, a digital process with minimal waiting. Prioritise locations with faster documentation TAT, shortlist two or three, and follow up with an immediate KYC and payment link. Convert on speed and convenience.

Price-based — “I need the best value”

Shift the conversation from price to value. Transparent pricing, relevant plans, verified documentation, end-to-end support. Offer two or three options at different price points, give a clear breakup of inclusions, and use discount only as a closing lever — never as the opening pitch. Convert on value, not just price.

Primary script
“I understand pricing is important. Before we compare numbers, let me understand exactly what you need — I want to make sure we’re comparing like for like.”

Location-based — “I need the right address”

Lead with coverage: 350+ locations across 20+ cities, all verified. Offer a comparison of relevant options rather than pushing one. Convert on relevance.

Direct, curious, confused, in a hurry

Direct — keep it short, crisp and factual. Curious — explain and educate with relevant detail. Confused — simplify, guide, and make a single clear recommendation. In a hurry — prioritise the key information and get to the point.

Same strategy. Same goal. Different communication.

    10

    Refunds and terms

    Say this before payment, never after

    Refund policy

    Refund window. A refund is possible only until the draft agreement is signed and approved by the space partner. After signing, no refund.

    Two government rejections. If the GST or company registration application is rejected by the government twice, the client may be eligible for a partial refund.

    Rejection letters. Must be submitted within 60 days of the payment date.

    Non-refundable components. Payment gateway charges and documentation charges are non-refundable in all cases, including approved refunds.

    Refund transparency script
    “Before you make the payment — just to be upfront: refunds are possible until the draft agreement is signed. After that point the space has already committed, so I want you to be comfortable before we proceed.”

    Key terms and conditions

    The plan is for the specific entity and signatory named in the agreement and is not transferable. Plans have a defined validity, typically one year, and renewal is required to maintain the registered address. Mail handling is included in all plans; call forwarding is not available. No modifications to standard documents are entertained post-payment. The same address can be used by multiple companies — this is standard practice and legally compliant. A virtual office plan does not entitle the client to use the physical workspace.

    Support. Post-sales queries on documentation, KYC and refunds go to supportvo@myhq.in.

    Negotiates further. “I understand. This is already the best price I can offer. What I can assure you is full transparency and compliance-ready documentation.”

    Compares with a competitor. “That is a fair comparison. The difference usually comes down to documentation quality and compliance integrity — is the cheaper space owned or leased? Is its GSTIN verified?”

    Large company wanting a manual invoice. “Of course — I can raise a manual invoice for NEFT or RTGS. Please note documentation begins only after the payment reflects, which adds about two business days.”

    Root ownership. You must communicate the T&Cs, refund policy, document finality and payment gateway charges before payment. Never after a dispute.
    12

    Closing

    Converting intent into a committed action

    Every closing conversation must end with a specific, dated next action. “I’ll get back to you” from the client is an open loop and should be closed before the call ends.

    Primary script
    “I will send the proposal to your email and WhatsApp. Once you have reviewed it, I can generate your payment link — shall I call you back at [specific time] tomorrow?”

    “Let me think about it.” “Absolutely — that is fair. Is there a specific concern I can address right now? Sometimes a quick answer saves you a day of thinking.”

    “I need to check with my partner or CA.” “Of course. I will send the full proposal so everyone can review it. When do you expect to hear back — today or tomorrow?”

    Urgency close, used compliantly. “I want to be transparent — the coupon code I mentioned expires [today / in 24 hours]. After that it is full price.” Only say this when it is true.

    If they do not pay on the call

    Confirm a specific follow-up date and time. Send the proposal within five minutes of the call ending, including plan details, pricing and sample documents. Update Zoho CRM with the follow-up date, call notes and stage status. Send a WhatsApp message confirming both the proposal and the agreed follow-up time.

    13

    The follow-up call

    Scripts, questions and objections

    The follow-up is not a reminder call. It is a structured sales conversation designed to surface the objection the client did not voice the first time.

    Primary script
    “Hi [Name]! This is [Your Name] from myHQ Virtual Office. We spoke on [Day] about your [City] requirement — do you have two minutes?”

    Did not open the proposal. “No problem at all — let me take two minutes to walk you through the most important highlights right now.”

    Opened it but did not respond. “I see you’ve had a chance to look at it. Was there anything that needed more clarity?”

    Unresponsive, third attempt or beyond. “Hi [Name] — I’ll keep this very short. I just want to make sure I haven’t missed something important. Is the requirement still active?” Opening with a pattern interrupt works better than another reminder.

    Rapport on a follow-up

    Use what they told you last time. “Last time you mentioned you’re setting up a Pvt. Ltd. / expanding to [City] for GST…” Personalised rapport shows you remembered the conversation, which signals professionalism.

    QuestionAnswer

    What if my CA says the documents aren’t enough?Encourage the CA to download samples before payment. Post-payment, no modifications are made.

    Objection handling — the A-C-E framework

    Acknowledge the concern — validate it, never dismiss it. Clarify the real issue by asking one more question after their first answer. Evidence — respond with proof rather than opinion: data, sample documents, success rates.

    Closing the follow-up

    Primary script
    “Perfect — I’m glad we’re aligned. Let me generate your payment link right now. I’ll send it on WhatsApp and email together.”

    If the client hesitates: “I’ll stay on the line — let me know when you have the link open and I can walk you through it.” Confirm payment completion before ending the call, then remind the client to complete KYC today. Update the CRM immediately and send the post-payment WhatsApp confirmation.

    The follow-up call has one goal: payment confirmation before the call ends. Everything else is preparation for that moment.
