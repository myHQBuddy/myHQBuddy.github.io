1 · How to use this chapter

Listen for the eleven stages, not the words.

These are unedited calls from the floor. They are not perfect scripts — they are real conversations where the framework works, and sometimes where it slips. Your job is to map what you hear back onto the stages from Chapter 2.

As you listen to each call, note: where did need analysis actually begin? Did the pitch come before or after the requirement was confirmed? What objection surfaced, and was it the real one?

Confidential — internal training use only. These recordings contain live client conversations. They are streamed inside this module and must not be shared, re-recorded, or taken outside myHQ.
