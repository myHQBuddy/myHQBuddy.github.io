Legality & Virtual Office Fundamentals

Is a virtual office actually legal for company registration in India?

Yes. Virtual offices are legally accepted in India for company registration and GST registration.

The Kerala High Court, in CIT vs. Sreekrishna Textiles, recognised the use of virtual office addresses for GST registration.

myHQ spaces are verified for MCA and GST requirements. A virtual office can be used for company registration, GST registration, official correspondence, and other business requirements.

Will MCA, GST and banks accept a virtual office address for my company registration, GST registration and current account?

Yes. MCA and GST authorities accept virtual office addresses when the required documents and address are compliant.

Banks can also accept a virtual office address for opening a current account, depending on their requirements.

myHQ provides documents such as:

  • Registered address certificate
  • Lease copy
  • Utility bill
  • Other required supporting documents

More than 60% of myHQ clients have successfully opened current accounts with banks such as HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, and YES Bank.

I have heard that virtual office addresses can get rejected during GST registration. How can you assure me that yours will be accepted?

GST registration can be rejected if the address or documents are not properly verified.

Before onboarding a space, myHQ checks:

  • Lease agreement
  • Physical presence of the space
  • Required compliance documents

myHQ has recorded a 95%+ success rate across 10,000+ registrations.

Sample documents can also be shared so that your CA can check them before you make the payment.

Your CA is right that rejection can happen in some cases.

The important point is that the quality and compliance of the address and documents matter.

myHQ partner spaces have:

This is why myHQ has maintained a 95%+ success rate.

The main concern is not the virtual office model itself, but whether the address and documents are properly verified.

Are there any types of businesses or industries where a virtual office address may not be accepted?

Virtual office addresses can be used for most standard businesses for GST and company registration.

However, some regulated industries may require a physical premises inspection or additional government approvals.

Examples include:

NBFCs

  • Pharma manufacturing
  • Food processing
  • Businesses that require specific government approvals

For most service businesses, startups, and e-commerce businesses, a virtual office can be used.

Share your business type with us so we can check the requirement for your specific case.

What is the actual difference between a virtual office and a registered office? Is there any additional compliance or risk involved?

A virtual office is a service that provides a professional business address along with required documents and support.

A registered office is the official address of a company that is used for MCA and GST records.

A virtual office can be used as a registered office if the address and documents meet the required compliance standards.

The important thing is to use a properly verified address and valid documents.

I already have a physical office. Why would I need a virtual office?

If your physical office is in one city but your business operates in other states, you may need a GST registration in those states.

A virtual office can provide an address for GST registration in another state without renting a full physical office.

This can help businesses that are expanding into multiple states while keeping their costs lower.

I can simply use my home address for GST registration. Why should I pay for a virtual office?

You can use your home address if you have the required documents and permissions.

However, many landlords do not provide an NOC for business registration. This can create problems during GST registration.

Using a virtual office can provide:

  • A professional commercial address
  • Required registration documents

NOC

  • Better privacy
  • Support during the registration process

It also avoids the need to use your personal home address for business registration.

Is a virtual office the same thing as a virtual assistant service or a coworking space?

No. These are different services.

Virtual Office: Provides a professional business address, mail handling, and compliance documents.

Virtual Assistant Service: Provides remote administrative or customer-support services.

Coworking Space: Provides a physical workspace, desk, internet, and office facilities.

A myHQ Virtual Office mainly provides the address and documents required for business purposes.

Meeting rooms and coworking facilities may be available separately at an additional cost.
